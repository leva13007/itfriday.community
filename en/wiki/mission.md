# Mission

**Online community for IT professionals worldwide — live, technical, and human in equal measure.**

## Background

Before 2022, the IT community lived offline: meetups, talks, live conversation afterwards.
Gatherings like these open the door to IT — through networking, talks, and a sense of belonging.

After COVID and the full-scale invasion of 2022, that disappeared.
People scattered across the world. Connections broke.

**This project is the answer to that void.**

IT Friday is a standalone project built from scratch. It is not a continuation, offshoot or part of any other community or meetup and has no organisational or legal ties to any of them.

## Mission

Bring together IT people who share [our values](/en/wiki/values) — regardless of country or passport — around a shared space where:

- You can learn something new about technology (without excessive academia)
- You can talk about real IT life — career, relocation, burnout
- You can find people on the same wavelength
- You can be a speaker or a listener — both roles are equal
- You can share not only successes but also failures and hard times

## What this is NOT

- ❌ Not a course or bootcamp
- ❌ Not a recruitment channel
- ❌ Not a conference with a programme committee
- ❌ Not an attempt to revive, replace or continue other communities, or to compete with them
