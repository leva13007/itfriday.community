## What we'll cover

### Part 1 — Why LinkedIn actually matters

- A LinkedIn profile is a search result, not a resume: a recruiter sees the card (photo + headline) first, and only then decides whether to open the profile
- Skills are SEO, not a trophy shelf: top skills should repeat the keywords in your headline
- Empty sections (Featured, Certifications, Recommendations) mean lost conversion from "shown in search" to "recruiter replies"

### Part 2 — Live audit and live fixes of my own profile

- I go through my own LinkedIn live against a checklist: photo, banner, headline, About, skills, experience, featured, certifications, recommendations
- Fixing the bugs I find, live, on the spot
- Comparing against great examples — Danny Thompson, Simon Høiberg, Cassidy Williams, Sarah Drasner

### Part 3 — Audience profile teardown + Q&A

- Send your profile in chat — I'll review it live using the same checklist
- At the end — a ready-made checklist you can apply the same night
