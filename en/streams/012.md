## What We'll Discuss

What problem InertiaJS solves and why it's called the "modern monolith" — a client-rendered SPA built directly on a server framework, no separate REST/GraphQL API layer.

- Comparing approaches: Inertia vs SPA+API, vs Next.js/Remix, vs plain MPA, vs Livewire
- Oleh's practical experience: when to reach for Inertia for Seed/Series A startups, and when not to
- Where Inertia breaks down at scale — coupling, SEO/SSR, server load
- Inertia v2 (2025): async core, prefetching, deferred props — and honest criticism of the approach
