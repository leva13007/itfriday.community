## What We'll Discuss

Vlad used to write in the IT Friday chat while he was looking for a job. Now he's found one and is here to tell how it went. Oleh is a web developer and still searching. Two experiences that fill in each other's gaps: how many applications it takes to get an offer, which channels actually work, what mobile and web technical interviews ask, how AI is changing requirements, and what to do when the search drags on.

- From first application to offer: numbers, channels, CV
- The worst and the best interview
- The market through mobile and web eyes: openings, technical rounds, take-home tasks, remote
- Is AI really eating jobs, and do interviewers ask about AI
- Advice for anyone searching right now
- Questions from chat
