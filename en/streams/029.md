## What We'll Discuss

> Change of format: Oleh Talanov unfortunately can't join us this time. The topic stays the same, but instead of a conversation with a CTO, it's an open conversation with you.

I'm a developer who works with AI agents every day, and I'll share how that actually looks for me. Then the floor is yours: want to speak? Join the **IT Friday voice room** on Discord (<a href="https://discord.gg/az53vrKQU" target="_blank" rel="noopener">link</a>) and I'll bring you on air. Prefer not to? Drop it in the chat, I read everything.

### What we'll cover

- How I actually use AI, and whether it made me faster
- Rules: how to set boundaries for an agent, NDAs and your employer's code in LLMs
- Who owns code written by an agent
- Skills: do you need to test them, and how to tell an agent has lost the plot
- Do you understand the code AI wrote for you a month ago?
- People: “AI layoffs”, juniors, and the pressure to use AI or fall behind
- Blitz round, me vs the chat: “AI is a bubble”, “I'll never write code by hand again”, “prompts only in English”
- Your questions and opinions, by voice on Discord or in the chat

The big question of the evening: is AI in the team a new set of rules for everyone, or just another overhyped tool?
