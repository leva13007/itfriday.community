## What We'll Discuss

We've done plenty of AI streams from the developer's side: tools, agents, skills, workflow. This time we switch seats. Oleh Talanov is the one deciding who gets hired, what the team is allowed to do with AI, and what to tell clients who ask “you use AI, so why isn't it cheaper?”.

A conversation between two practitioners: an agency CTO and a developer who works with agents every day.

> Oleh is in Zaporizhzhia, where Russian strikes keep knocking out the power. If the connection drops, we have a backup plan and the stream goes on.

### What we'll cover

- How we actually use AI: tools, the working day, the team
- Did projects really get faster, and did clients notice
- Team rules: client code in LLMs, NDAs, CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md in repos
- Who owns code written by an agent
- Skills: we'll show ours, and whether you need to test them
- When you delegate code to agents, do you lose your bearings in your own project?
- People: “AI layoffs”, juniors, and the pressure to use AI or fall behind
- Blitz round: “AI is a bubble”, “I'll never write code by hand again”, “prompts only in English”
- Questions from chat

The big question of the evening: is AI in the team a new set of rules for everyone, or just another overhyped tool?
