## What we'll cover

A hiring manager's view of structured behavioral interviews: how to build them around the STAR method and a competency model, and how to reduce bias when evaluating candidates. Ihor doesn't have professional IT experience yet — he researched and systematized the STAR methodology as a student of the topic, not as a practicing hiring manager, and shares it with that honest framing.

- Behavioral vs. situational questions — where real actions show up, not just intentions
- The STAR framework (Situation → Task → Action → Result), walked through step by step with an example
- Competency-based question banks for comparing candidates fairly
- Reducing bias in evaluation (halo effect, first impressions) + cross-cultural hiring
- Live and unscripted at the end: Ihor puts a real behavioral question to Oleh, live on air
