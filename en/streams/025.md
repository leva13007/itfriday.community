## What We'll Discuss

Part one of a long-awaited conversation with HR. Many of the questions came anonymously from our community: why there's silence for two weeks after an interview, who writes job posts with 20 technologies, why strong candidates get rejected, and what to do when a job search has lasted a year and a half. Not defending recruiters or blaming candidates — figuring out where the process really breaks and what each side can influence.

- Behind the scenes: silence after interviews, ghosting, how many rounds are enough
- Job posts with 20 technologies: what's actually must-have
- Why strong developers get rejected, and which red flags are real vs myths
- "Why are you changing jobs?" when the honest answer is salary
- Layoffs, career gaps, a 1.5-year search: where the funnel breaks and what to do tomorrow
- Money: who names the number first, and whether to accept a counteroffer
- AI and hiring: ATS filters, ChatGPT-written CVs, and whether juniors are still needed
- Rapid-fire round and questions from chat
